## About Me

I'm a **full-stack developer with 7+ years of experience** building production web platforms, mobile apps, AI-powered chatbots and automation bots. I founded **[WebAra](https://web-ara.com)**, a software studio in Hamedan, Iran, where I lead the design, development and delivery of products for clients across e-commerce, tourism and education.

- 🏗️ I build end-to-end: database design, APIs, admin panels, responsive front-ends and deployment
- 🤖 I integrate AI into real products: LLM chatbots, AI agents and retrieval-based assistants
- 🛒 I've shipped e-commerce, booking and content platforms used by real customers every day
- 🌍 Open to remote roles and international opportunities
